Visual theme of existing Federated Learning educational web application using a **Minimalist Warm Neutral** aesthetic.

The app uses Sunset Vibes color palette, but looks much less colorful. The overall design is clean, modern, calm, sophisticated, and slightly playful.

### Color Palette

- Main Background: `#F1F1EF` (Light Gray)
- Cards and Content Panels: `#FFFDF7` (Warm Off-White)
- Primary Text and Buttons: `#242424` (Charcoal)
- Secondary Text: `#666666` (Medium Gray)
- Borders: `#E0E0DE`
- Bright Yellow Accent: `#FFDB00`
- Deep Orange Accent: `#F58A00`

### Design Rules

1. Use light gray as the dominant background throughout the entire application.
2. Use warm off-white for cards, content containers, experiment panels, and dashboard sections.
3. Use charcoal for typography, navigation, icons, and primary buttons.
4. Use bright yellow and deep orange **only in small, intentional areas**, such as:
   - Small accent lines under module cards.
   - Tiny status indicators and icons.
   - Selected tabs and progress indicators.
   - Achievement highlights.
   - Specific chart lines or data points.
5. Do not use bright yellow or orange as large section backgrounds, hero backgrounds, or full card backgrounds.
6. Use mostly neutral-colored learning module cards, with subtle color accents to differentiate modules.
7. Maintain generous whitespace, subtle borders, rounded corners, and minimal shadows.
8. Keep dashboards and model-training visualizations professional and easy to read.
9. Use accessible contrast for all text and interactive controls; never use accent color alone to communicate meaning.
10. Avoid gradients, neon colors, excessive decoration, and overly saturated UI components.

### Desired Visual Balance

- 55% Light Gray
- 30% Warm Off-White
- 10% Charcoal
- 3% Bright Yellow
- 2% Deep Orange

These percentages are approximate visual guidelines, not strict measurements.

### Important

The website should resemble a modern Scandinavian-inspired university educational platform, not a colorful children's learning website.

Preserve all existing functionality, experiments, page structure, and navigation. Update the styling consistently across the three federated learning modules using centralized design tokens.
